CASTLE 6th Conference 2026 Promotes Global Transformation

Jakarta, August 1, 2026
CASTLE 6th Conference 2026 Virtual Session via Zoom Workplace

Kelompok Peneliti Muda (KPM) of Universitas Negeri Jakarta, in collaboration with the Directorate of Student Affairs & Alumni UNJ, held the 6th CASTLE Conference 2026 virtually via Zoom Workplace.

With the theme "Driving Global Transformation in an Era of Uncertainty Through Intelligence, Innovation, and Collaborative Solutions," the conference focused on strategic issues, innovation, and global collaboration.

Through this event, KPM UNJ aims to foster knowledge exchange, innovation, and collaboration among students and researchers across disciplines and countries.
