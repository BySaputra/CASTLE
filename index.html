What is CASTLE?

About CASTLE 2026

CASTLE (Conference on Social, Science, Technology, Language, and Education Research) is an international academic forum designed to bring together scholars, researchers, and practitioners from diverse disciplinary backgrounds. The conference fosters interdisciplinary dialogue and disseminates cutting-edge research findings to promote academic excellence.

Our mission is to create a dynamic platform where academic excellence meets practical application, enabling researchers to share innovative insights and collaborate across boundaries. CASTLE 2026 welcomes contributions from various fields including technology, education, social sciences, language studies, and related domains.

Target Participants

  • Academic researchers and scholars
  • Graduate and postgraduate students
  • Industry professionals
  • Policy makers and practitioners

Conference Purpose

  • Share research findings and innovations
  • Foster interdisciplinary collaboration
  • Build international academic networks
  • Advance knowledge in key fields
