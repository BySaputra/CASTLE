Conference Overview

Advancing Research, Innovation, and Global Academic Collaboration

CASTLE 6.0 is the Conference on Social, Science, Technology, Language, and Education Research, an academic conference that brings together researchers, educators, students, and professionals to share scholarly work and interdisciplinary perspectives.

The conference serves as a platform for academic discussion, research presentation, and scientific collaboration across various fields, while encouraging innovation and global knowledge exchange in the era of rapid transformation.

Conference Theme

Driving Global Transformation in an Era of Uncertainty Through Intelligence, Innovation, and Collaborative Solutions

Research Areas

Education

Research in learning innovation, pedagogy, curriculum, and educational technology.

Science

Scientific studies, discovery, experimentation, and interdisciplinary science research.

Technology

Digital innovation, artificial intelligence, IoT, engineering, and emerging technologies.

Social

Social transformation, culture, policy, communication, and human-centered studies.

Language

Linguistics, communication, translation, literacy, and language education.
